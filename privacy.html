BitStream Audio

Privacy Policy · Effective 14 August 2026

BitStream Audio is an independent music label. This policy covers the applications the label operates on the Meta platform, which are used solely to publish the label's own content to the label's own accounts and to manage the label's own advertising.

What these applications do

The label operates two Meta applications:

  • One publishes the label's own videos to the label's own Instagram account.
  • One manages advertising for the label's own posts, through an advertising account the label owns.

Both act only on accounts and assets belonging to BitStream Audio. Neither is offered to, installed by, or usable by any other person or organisation.

Information collected

These applications do not collect personal information from anyone. They have no user-facing interface, no sign-up, no login for third parties, and no mechanism by which another person could provide information to them.

The only credentials involved are the label's own access tokens, which authorise the applications to act on the label's own accounts. They are stored locally on the operator's machine and are not transmitted anywhere other than to Meta.

Information shared

Nothing is shared with third parties. No data is sold, licensed, transferred or disclosed. There are no analytics providers, advertising networks or data processors involved beyond Meta itself, which receives the content the label publishes and the advertising instructions the label issues.

Aggregate advertising statistics

Meta reports aggregate, anonymised statistics about advertising performance, such as the number of impressions, the countries in which they were served, and broad age bands. This information is produced by Meta, contains no identifiable individuals, and is used only to judge whether advertising is worth continuing.

Retention

Because no personal information is collected, there is none to retain. Access tokens expire on Meta's schedule and are replaced.

Children

These applications are not directed at children and collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the label's use of the Meta platform changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and its effective date revised.
